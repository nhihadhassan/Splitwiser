# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Nhihad and trusted travel companions reviewing shared trip expenses on desktop
and mobile. The primary job is to compare a detailed trip log against card and
cash activity, resolve differences confidently, and preserve a trustworthy
record of every decision.

## Product Purpose

Splitwiser is a personal shared-expense ledger for trips and everyday groups.
The reconciliation workspace should make dense financial review feel calm and
legible: find a charge, select related records, compare totals, explain a
difference, and close a trip without losing source detail.

## Brand Personality

Warm, precise, composed. The product should feel like a carefully kept account
book rather than enterprise treasury software or a generic white dashboard.

## Anti-references

- Bright white accounting grids pasted into the dark application shell.
- Generic fintech navy, neon crypto interfaces, or decorative glass effects.
- Tiny data tables that trade readability for maximum row count.
- Card collections that fragment one continuous ledger into disconnected tiles.
- Ambiguous automation that hides why or how transactions were matched.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the ledger visually continuous and make source-to-source comparison the
   dominant structure.
2. Use hierarchy to reduce cognitive load before adding more controls.
3. Keep every financial decision explicit, reversible, and easy to audit.
4. Preserve the established Warm Ledger language across every new workflow.
5. Let dense data remain readable through stable columns, generous row rhythm,
   and progressive disclosure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic labels, touch-safe
mobile controls, reduced-motion support, and status cues that do not rely on
color alone. Avoid horizontal overflow on mobile reconciliation views.
